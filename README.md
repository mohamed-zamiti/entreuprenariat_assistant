# StartupGuide AI
### An AI-Powered Entrepreneurship Assistant for Beginners in Tunisia

## Overview

StartupGuide AI is a web-based AI assistant designed to help aspiring entrepreneurs, startup founders, and small business owners in Tunisia navigate the challenges of starting and managing a business.

The platform uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to provide educational, context-aware, and source-backed answers using entrepreneurship resources, business textbooks, and official Tunisian documentation.

The goal is to make entrepreneurship knowledge more accessible to people who have business ideas but lack experience in business management, e-commerce, marketing, finance, or administrative procedures.

## Problem Statement

Starting a business can be overwhelming for beginners. Many aspiring entrepreneurs struggle with questions such as:

- How do I turn my idea into a real business?
- How can I validate my business idea?
- How do I find customers and sell my products?
- How can I start an e-commerce business?
- How do I calculate costs, prices, and profit?
- What administrative procedures are required in Tunisia?
- What are the legal and tax obligations of a business?
- How can I obtain funding for my startup?

Although this information exists across books, official websites, and administrative documents, it is often fragmented, technical, and difficult for beginners to navigate.

StartupGuide AI aims to centralize this knowledge through an accessible conversational interface.

## Project Objectives

- Provide beginner-friendly entrepreneurship guidance.
- Help users understand how to create and manage a business.
- Support e-commerce and digital marketing beginners.
- Explain fundamental financial and accounting concepts.
- Simplify access to Tunisian administrative and legal information.
- Provide reliable answers supported by retrieved sources.
- Guide users through different stages of their entrepreneurial journey.

## Main Features

### 1. AI Business Assistant
An intelligent chatbot that answers entrepreneurship-related questions using a curated knowledge base.

### 2. Business Idea Validation
Guidance on identifying market opportunities, defining target customers, analyzing competitors, and developing a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

### 3. E-commerce & Sales Guidance
Educational assistance covering online selling, product pricing, customer acquisition, digital marketing, and e-commerce strategies.

### 4. Financial & Business Management
Explanations of essential concepts such as revenue, profit, expenses, cash flow, budgeting, and business planning.

### 5. Tunisian Administrative & Legal Guidance
Access to source-backed information about business registration, startup regulations, taxation, and relevant administrative procedures in Tunisia.

Legal and tax responses are informational and should reference applicable official sources rather than replace professional advice.

### 6. Source-Based Answers
The chatbot retrieves relevant information from its knowledge base and provides references whenever available.

### 7. User Accounts & Chat History
Authentication and personalized conversation history to allow users to revisit previous discussions.

## Target Audience

- Aspiring entrepreneurs with little or no business experience.
- Students interested in entrepreneurship.
- First-time startup founders.
- Beginners entering e-commerce.
- Small business owners seeking educational guidance.
- Individuals interested in understanding Tunisian business procedures.

## Knowledge Base

The RAG knowledge base is designed around nine main categories:

| Category | Topics |
|---|---|
| Business Fundamentals | Entrepreneurship, business types, business operations |
| Idea Validation | Market research, competitors, MVP |
| Business Planning | Business models, feasibility, business plans |
| Sales & Marketing | Branding, pricing, promotion, customer acquisition |
| E-commerce | Online stores, digital marketing, customer experience |
| Finance & Accounting | Costs, revenue, profit, budgeting, cash flow |
| Business Management | Operations, organization, decision-making |
| Tunisian Regulations | Business registration, taxation, legal procedures |
| Startup Ecosystem | Startup Act, funding, entrepreneurship programs |
